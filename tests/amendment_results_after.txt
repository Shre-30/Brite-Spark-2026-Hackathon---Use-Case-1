expected (after): Should answer: no, per new Â§10.5.3A
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The manual contains conflicting provisions on this question. No single answer can be given without resolving the discrepancy; both provisions are set out below.

Nature of the conflict: §10.5.3A states that a sanction must not be imposed if the change of circumstances would have increased the award, while §10.5.3 states that a sanction must not be imposed if the household includes a dependent child under 2 or if the recipient requires assistance with two or more activities of daily living. However, §10.5.3A does not provide an exception for dependent children or assistance with activities of daily living, suggesting that a sanction could be imposed in these cases if the change of circumstances would have increased the award, which contradicts §10.5.3.

[§10.5.3A] A sanction must not be imposed in respect of a failure to report where the change of circumstances in question would have increased the award. *(inserted by Amendment 2026-01, effective 1 March 2026)*

[§10.5.3] A sanction must not be imposed where the household includes a dependent
child under the age of 2, or where the recipient has been assessed as requiring
assistance with two or more activities of daily living.

Recommended action: refer this question to a policy supervisor for clarification. Neither provision should be relied upon in isolation until the conflict is resolved.
